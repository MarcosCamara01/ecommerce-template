Stripe/Storage/SMTP test adapters on loopback only. No hosted credentials or
payloads are recorded here.

and `RUNTIME_TEST_ADMIN_DATABASE_URL` for cleanup. Both URLs must address the
same disposable loopback protocol, host, port, and database; only credentials
may differ.
